Privacy Policy

Farm Manager & Digital Farm Book (Android app, package com.ayoob.farmmanager)

Your farm records stay on your device

Effective date: 16 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 16 September 2026

This policy explains what the Farm Manager & Digital Farm Book app (“the app”, “we”) does with information when you use it. It applies to the Android app distributed through Google Play and to the web version of the same app.

1.Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your farm records.
  • Everything you enter — farms, fields, crops, workers, wages, machinery, fuel, livestock, inventory, income and expenses — is saved locally on your device.
  • The app works fully offline. No internet connection is required to use your farm book.
  • Backups and exports (PDF, CSV, Excel, JSON) are created only when you tap the button, and are saved or shared only where you choose.

2.Information you enter (stored on your device)

The app is a digital logbook, so the data you type stays under your control. This includes:

  • Farm and field details, including names, locations, owner names and area in acres or hectares
  • Crop cycles, varieties, sowing and harvest dates and expected yields
  • Worker names, phone numbers you choose to enter, roles, wage rates and task logs
  • Machinery, fuel purchases, livestock records and fertilizer / agricultural medicine inventory
  • Income, expenses, currency settings and app preferences

This information is stored in the app's local storage on your phone. We have no server and no access to it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that information is permanently deleted, so please create a backup first if you want to keep it.

3.Information we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, files, calendar, microphone or camera data. It contains no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK.

4.Advertising

The app may display advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. If ads are enabled in the version you install, Google may collect and process limited information such as your device advertising identifier, device type, coarse region, IP address and ad-interaction events in order to serve and measure ads. This processing is carried out by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your farm records are never sent to Google, advertisers or any other third party. You can limit ad personalisation at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or reset your advertising ID there.

5.Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Internet / network stateOnly to load advertisements, if ads are enabled. The farm book itself works offline.
Storage / file access (when prompted)Only when you choose to save or open a backup, CSV, Excel or PDF export.

The app requests no other sensitive permissions.

6.Backups, exports and sharing

When you use Backup & restore or Export, the app creates a file containing the records you entered and hands it to your device so you can save it or send it through an app of your choice (for example Files, Drive, email or WhatsApp). Once a file leaves the app, it is handled by the service or app you selected, under that provider's own privacy policy. We never receive a copy.

7.Children's privacy

The app is a business and record-keeping tool intended for farmers and farm businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8.Data security

Because your records never leave your device unless you export them yourself, the main protection is your device itself. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping regular backups using the built-in backup feature.

9.Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: all your data is visible and editable inside the app.
  • Deletion: delete individual records, use More → Backup & restore → Erase all data, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Portability: export your data at any time as JSON, CSV, Excel or PDF.

Since we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on your behalf.

10.Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a cloud sync feature is added — we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
